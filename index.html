● FEATURED RESEARCH INQUIRY Digital Image Forensics

PhotoDNA — Detection of Manipulation in Photos

A comprehensive research inquiry investigating digital photograph authenticity, altered visual media detection, and the forensic examination of digital photographs.

// CRITICAL FORENSIC DISTINCTION:

PhotoDNA is a recognized perceptual image hashing / robust matching technology designed to identify known illicit imagery, rather than a standalone pixel-level manipulation detector.

This research evaluates both the utility of perceptual hashing frameworks (such as PhotoDNA) and passive image tampering detection techniques—including Error Level Analysis (ELA), copy-move clone detection, Color Filter Array (CFA) interpolation artifacts, and noise variance analysis—within end-to-end digital forensic verification pipelines.
